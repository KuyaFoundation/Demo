  • By electing the cash alternative, the Winner releases the Organiser from any obligation in relation to the original non-cash prize, including its custom build, transport, registration, and insurance.

11.Taxes

Under Section 24(B)(1) of the National Internal Revenue Code (as amended by the TRAIN Law), prizes exceeding ₱10,000 awarded to Philippine resident individuals are subject to a 20% final withholding tax. For non-resident aliens not engaged in trade or business in the Philippines, a final withholding tax of 25% may apply (per BIR Revenue Memorandum Circular No. 5-2024 and successor issuances).

The Organiser shoulders the tax. All applicable final withholding tax on prizes is grossed up and paid by the Organiser to the BIR. The Winner receives the prize value as advertised, without further deduction.

The gross-up is computed per BIR formula: Gross-Up Value = Prize FMV ÷ (1 − Tax Rate); Tax = Gross-Up Value × Tax Rate. For example, a ₱100,000 advertised cash prize at 20% rate results in a gross-up of ₱125,000 declared to the BIR, with ₱25,000 remitted as withholding tax and ₱100,000 paid to the Winner.

For each Draw, the Organiser will:

  • File BIR Form 0619-F and remit the withholding tax on or before the 10th day of the month following prize handover;
  • Issue BIR Form 2306 (Certificate of Final Tax Withheld at Source) to the Winner within twenty (20) days after the close of the calendar quarter in which the prize was awarded;
  • File BIR Form 1604-F (Annual Information Return of Final Income Taxes Withheld) with the alphabetical list of Winners by 31 January of the following calendar year.

Documentary Stamp Tax under Section 188 of the NIRC may apply to ticket issuance (currently ₱0.20 per ₱200 face value, or fraction thereof). Where DST applies, it is collected and remitted by the Organiser and included in the published ticket price — there is no additional DST charge to the Participant at point of sale.
