The Roadmap

Three islands. One mission.

The Boracay template, then deployed across the Philippines' three most visited island destinations.

Year 1 · Phase 1

Boracay — Launch Chapter

Foundation operational from Month 1. Two response vehicles (Boracay + Caticlan), sea ambulance acquired by Month 5, USAR team certified by Month 8, full foundation crew taking over by Month 12. 24-hour professional coverage.

Year 1–2 · Phase 1

Kuya LifeSaver Critical Care Unit

Named donation to Ciriaco S. Tirol Hospital — currently upgrading to Level 1 with a new 5-storey building. ICU-grade beds, ventilators, multiparameter monitors. The most serious cases treated on the island.

Year 2 · Phase 2

Siargao Chapter

Boracay template deployed. Local board, local USAR team recruited, foundation-funded vessel and vehicles. Same model, same brand, different island.

Year 2 · Phase 2

El Nido Chapter

Third island chapter. Palawan geography, longer water crossings, vessel specification adjusted. Three Kuya LifeSaver chapters simultaneously operational across the country's top tourist islands.

Year 3 · Phase 3

Dedicated Medevac Helicopter

Platform revenue at national scale funds a dedicated helicopter retainer through MedicAir / PhilJets. The Westpac model, on Philippine waters.
